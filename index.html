Download the data

  • market_monthly.csv monthly totals
  • brand_monthly.csv by brand
  • model_monthly.csv by model
  • variant_monthly.csv variant names as recorded by the DLT
  • province_monthly.csv registrations by province per month (provinces.csv province codes and regions)
  • summaries/areas/ brand/model units by area (country, region, province)
  • brands.csv brands, IDs and parent-company country
  • data/registrations_by_fuel/ brand units by fuel type and province (fuel_types.csv)
  • population.csv population by province, from the Department of Provincial Administration
  • prices.csv prices from official websites, with sources
  • data/ row-level data, brand/model IDs and the source registry
